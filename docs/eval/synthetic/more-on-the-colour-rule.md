Following on from what I said about the colour thing earlier — I want to add to that rather than start over. The point I made was that coral means Switchyard and gold means Amber anywhere either one resolves, and that it's an estate rule and not a per-app choice. What I didn't say, and what I think is actually the more useful half, is why: it's so that you can glance at a dense page with a dozen references on it and know what kind of thing each one is without reading a single label. The colour is doing the work the label would otherwise have to do. Which also means you don't get to spend those two colours on anything else, ever, in any app, even when it would look nice.
