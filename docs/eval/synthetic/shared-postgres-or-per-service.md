Worth actually talking through: everything on the box shares one Postgres, with a database and a role each. That's been fine and it means one thing to back up, one thing to tune, one thing to keep patched. But it also means one restart takes down every service at once, and I've now got two services where the isolation between them is enforced by a grant rather than by a boundary you could see. The alternative is a Postgres per service, which is more containers, more memory, more backup targets, and honestly more chances for one of them to be the one I forgot to upgrade. I don't have a strong feeling. It probably depends on whether the shared instance ever actually becomes the thing that's down.
