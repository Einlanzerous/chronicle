How much CTA history is actually worth keeping. A year of hourly resolution sounds great right up until you look at what that costs per line per station, and I don't know that anyone has ever asked a question that needed more than about ninety days at that granularity. The obvious move is roll up anything older than three months to daily and keep that forever, which is small. The thing that makes me hesitate is that the interesting questions are the seasonal ones — what does a January look like versus a July — and those are exactly the ones you can't ask if you rolled the resolution away. So there's a real tension and I don't think I can resolve it without knowing what I'd actually want to ask.
