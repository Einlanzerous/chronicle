Open question about the tablet. Right now it's entirely reactive — I say something, it answers. The interesting version is one where it volunteers, so it can say hey, this thing you were watching finished deploying, or you haven't been to the gym this week. But the moment it speaks unprompted it's a device that makes noise in my house on its own judgement, and the bar for that is enormously higher than the bar for answering a question. Get it wrong twice and I turn it off permanently. I lean towards letting it push to my phone instead of speaking, at least at first, but I'm genuinely not sure that isn't just avoiding the interesting problem.
