I want to be careful about the word disposable because I've been using it for two different things and they are not the same. Tier one is disposable because you can delete it and it rebuilds itself from something that lives elsewhere. That's a property of where it came from. Audio is not that — audio gets pruned at thirty days by policy, and once it's gone nothing brings it back. Calling both of those disposable is how you end up with a pruner that deletes something irreplaceable and thinks it's doing routine maintenance. Different words, or at least different sentences.
