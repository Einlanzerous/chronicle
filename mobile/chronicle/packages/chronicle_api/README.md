# chronicle_api
Voice-note ingestion into a notes and discussion wiki. A memo is captured,
transcribed on the shared ASR service, routed by the Scribe into a note, a
ticket or a discussion, and lands in a corpus that links out to Switchyard
and Amber rather than absorbing them.

**Two tiers, and they do not share a store.** Tier 1 is the estate's account
of what exists — generated, regenerable, never hand-edited. Tier 2 is what a
person said or wrote — memos, transcripts, notes, discussions — and none of
it can be rebuilt. They are reached by different code paths through
different database roles.

**References are linked, never copied.** A ticket or an Amber item resolves
at render time and is never written into Chronicle's tables. Resolution
therefore has its own endpoint: a note payload carries the references its
text names and nothing an upstream said about them.
