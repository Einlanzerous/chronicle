Credentials live in Signet and they get injected at run time. They do not live in a compose file, they do not live in a checked-in env file, and they do not get echoed into a log line to check they're set. If you want to verify a container has the right config, check that the variable is present, not what it holds. I've leaked things doing exactly that and the annoying part is that a leak in a log is a leak in every aggregator that scrapes the log, so it isn't one place you have to clean up, it's four.
