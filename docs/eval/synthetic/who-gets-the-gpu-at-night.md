There's no arbitration for the GPU and it's going to bite. Transcription wants it, the routing model wants it, and anything I add later that runs a local model wants it, and none of them go through the same path so none of them can queue behind each other. The transcription service can single-flight its own work but it has no idea the card is busy doing something else entirely. So either something has to own the device and everything asks it, or I schedule by convention and accept that two things occasionally fight. Convention is obviously less work and obviously the one that fails at three in the morning. Worth talking through before I add a third consumer.
